The Lead · Culture & Technology

Vice City, At Last: GTA VI Opens Its Gates to the World

Auckland — Thirteen years after its predecessor, Grand Theft Auto VI launched today, unlocking at midnight local time in one time zone after another, New Zealand first. Queues formed outside shops from Milan to Mumbai, and by midday in Paris some 2.3 million people were watching others play it live on Twitch.

The first reviews were close to unanimous: an average of 99 out of 100 on Metacritic, among the highest ever recorded. Players return to Vice City, in the state of Leonida, as Jason and Lucia, a couple whose easy score goes wrong. The game is out on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S; no PC date has been announced.

Politics · Shenzhen

Three Nuclear Powers, One Table

Donald Trump, Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin meet today in Shenzhen in the first trilateral summit of the three leaders, held beside the APEC gathering. Trump wants nuclear arms control at the centre of the talks; Beijing, whose arsenal is far smaller, has resisted joining any three-way negotiation. Analysts expect pageantry more than agreements.

Markets · New York

Anthropic’s IPO Is the Biggest Ever

The maker of the Claude AI models priced its shares at $83 and raised $81.3 billion, the largest stock-market debut on record, ahead of SpaceX’s offering in June. The price values Anthropic at roughly $1.5 trillion. Its shares begin trading on Nasdaq today under the ticker ANT, a first test of investors’ appetite for frontier AI.

Weather · India

Cyclone Akvan Nears the Coast

Cyclone Akvan is expected to cross India’s east coast between Machilipatnam and Puri, and disaster-response teams across Andhra Pradesh and Odisha are on alert. Railway officials reviewed preparations along the threatened corridor as the storm churned over the Bay of Bengal, with heavy rain and high winds forecast at landfall.

Science · Deep Space

Voyager 1 Reaches One Light-Day From Earth

NASA’s Voyager 1, launched in 1977, has become the first human-made object to sit a full light-day from home: its radio signals, travelling at the speed of light, now take a whole day to reach Earth. Nearly half a century on, it is still reporting back from interstellar space.

Sport · Cricket, Hobart

Duckett’s 133 Seals the Series for England

Ben Duckett struck 133 as England posted 328 and beat Australia by 97 runs in Hobart, taking the one-day series 2–1 in the deciding match. A sharp new-ball spell broke the home side’s chase early, and the hosts never recovered.
